HOW TO USE

01Open a webpage and click the loca.arc extension icon.

02Enter a word or phrase, then press Enter or click FIND.

03Use the ↑ / ↓ buttons or a result row to move between matches.

04Click RESET SEARCH to clear loca.arc highlights and start again.

05For snippets or code, open “Search pasted text / code instead”.

Some protected browser pages and complex editors do not expose searchable text.
